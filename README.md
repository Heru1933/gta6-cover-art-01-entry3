Neon Vice is an offline cover-art maker that turns your own photos into GTA-poster-style cover art. Drop in photos (or start from the four built-in samples), arrange them across six panel layouts — Mosaic (7 panels), Poster (hero + 3), Quad, Trio, Duo and Solo — with cream keylines and soft panel shadows, then finish the look with one of five colour grades (Vice Sunset, Neon Night, Golden Hour, Noir, Clean), film grain and vignette, and an optional striped retro-sun overlay. Every panel is editable on its own: click to select it, drag to pan its photo, scroll or use the slider to zoom, switch between Fill and Fit, re-centre it, cycle its photo, or fall back to a hand-drawn sunset scene. Letter the cover with an editable title, tagline and foot line in the vendored Anton and Bebas Neue typefaces — the title auto-fits, takes a sunset, gold, pink or white fill, and sits at the top or bottom edge. Export is one click to PNG at 1080×1080, 1080×1350 or 1920×1080, and the preview canvas is always the exact export resolution, so what you see is the file you get. The app is fully static with zero network at runtime: photos never leave your machine, and there is no watermark and no Rockstar artwork anywhere in it.

## Run it

```sh
mkdir -p out
MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```

Open `app/index.html` in a browser to use the cover maker itself.
